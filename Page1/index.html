May 20th

Dear Diary

I steped my foot onto this land. They called me foreigner - I mean the land called me this. Sandra picked me up from the airport. She had a dog, Beau. I saw his eyes. I read from the glimpse of sadness that his owners were too busy, I wish he was a cat. So he won't feel bad waiting at home alone the whole day.

I am a bird longing to be free, to get my skin burnt by el sol del mediterraneo. To melt the eternal snow Chicago put on me before I departed.
